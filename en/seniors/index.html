Discounts and cards

This is what the Philharmonic’s ticket regulations say. Show the document for your discount to the staff at the hall entrance.

  • Reduced ticket from age 60

    For people over 60, pensioners and disability pensioners. No card needed: an ID showing your age or a pensioner’s card is enough.

  • Szczecin Senior Card: 30 zł

    For selected concerts you can buy one ticket for 30 zł, whatever the seat. One ticket per card. At the box office and online. The card is issued by Szczecin City Hall.

    Concerts with the 30 zł Senior Card price:

    • Wednesday, 25 November 2026, 19:00: From the four corners of the world
    • Saturday, 30 January 2027, 19:00: Symphonic Monuments | Debussy
    • Friday, 12 February 2027, 19:00: Quo vadis?
    • Friday, 12 March 2027, 19:00: Collegium Marianum | Once upon a time...
    • Friday, 19 March 2027, 19:00: Before the Silence | Beethoven 200
    • Wednesday, 28 April 2027, 19:00: The Power of Contrasts
    • Friday, 21 May 2027, 19:00: Beyond Time
    • Friday, 4 June 2027, 19:00: This is the Moment

  • Other senior cards

    The regulations only mention the Szczecin Senior Card. The national Ogólnopolska Karta Seniora is not listed, but the 60+ reduced ticket needs no card at all.

  • Companion

    The regulations provide a companion ticket for people with a certified disability: the companion then buys a reduced ticket. Without a certificate a companion buys an ordinary ticket (reduced if they are 60 or over).

  • Coming as a group?

    Group tickets apply from 15 people for one event. Email the box office (date, number of people, name, phone) and it will confirm the booking.

  • Season ticket: 15 % off

    Buying at least 7 tickets for different concerts? Each ticket is then 15 % cheaper.

When the Senior Card does not apply

The Senior Card discount does not apply to the season opening and closing, presidential, New Year’s Eve and New Year concerts. Ask the box office about a reduced ticket for those evenings.

Proposal (concept, not yet in place)

Mornings for senior clubs and Universities of the Third Age

Idea: regular cooperation with Universities of the Third Age and senior clubs: Konsonanse dates announced early, a paper programme by post or at the club, and one phone number for group sign-ups. Group tickets from 15 people already exist today.

Getting here

Step by step, from your front door to your seat.

  1. Address

    ul. Małopolska 48, 70-515 Szczecin. The main entrance is on Małopolska Street, level with the pavement, no steps.

  2. By tram, right to the door

    Stop “Filharmonia”, tram 1. About 1 minute’s walk to the entrance.

  3. Other lines: Plac Rodła

    About 3 minutes on foot. Trams 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 11 and 12, buses 59, 68, 74, 86, 101, 107, 530, A, B and C.

  4. By taxi

    Give the driver the address ul. Małopolska 48. It is the address of the main entrance.

  5. By car

    The underground car park (154 spaces) opens 60 minutes before a concert and is free with a ticket. Each of its 2 levels has 2 spaces for disabled drivers. A lift goes straight from the car park to the foyer.

  6. In the foyer

    The box office, cloakroom and café are on the ground floor. The cloakroom is straight ahead of the entrance; it opens 60 minutes before a concert and closes 30 minutes after it.

  7. A lift to both halls

    Lifts serve every floor and have Braille and voice announcements. The chamber hall and the stalls entrances of the symphony hall are on the 1st floor.

  8. Toilets

    Accessible toilets are on the ground floor and the 1st floor.

  9. A comfortable seat and help

    Would you like a seat close to the entrance, or do you need help? Tell the box office when you buy. Staff are trained and can provide an assistant during the concert.

  10. Induction loop

    The accessibility statement says an induction loop is installed, but not in which hall. Ask the box office whether it covers your seat.

  11. When to arrive

    Ideally about 20 minutes before the concert. The hall opens 30 minutes before the start. One bell: 15 minutes to go; three bells: time to take your seat.

Questions and answers

The questions we hear most before a first visit, and every visit after.

Can I return or exchange a ticket?

Tickets cannot be exchanged. Refunds are only given if the event is cancelled – at the box office, with the receipt or invoice. Online tickets are non-refundable except for cancellation; money goes back to the account or card you paid with. Complaints are handled within 14 working days.

Source: bilety.filharmonia.szczecin.pl/index/regulamin-sprzedazy.html

What if I'm late?

Once the concert starts the doors are closed. Ushers will let you in at a suitable moment – sometimes between pieces, sometimes only at the interval. The regulations allow late-comers to be refused; entry may then be possible at the interval.

Source: bilety.filharmonia.szczecin.pl/index/regulamin-sprzedazy.html

Can I buy a wheelchair space online?

Yes. The symphonic hall has 4 wheelchair spaces (marked on the seat plan), ticket 10 zł. You can pick the companion seat next to it and buy both in one transaction. In the chamber hall wheelchair spaces are booked via the box office.

Source: bilety.filharmonia.szczecin.pl/index/dostepnosc.html

Can I get assistance during my visit?

Yes. Staff are trained to assist disabled visitors. Tell the box office about your needs when buying or before you come and an assistant will be provided during the concert. The accessibility statement lists 08:00–16:00 for this number, but the box office answers during its own hours (Tue–Fri 12–18, Sat 12–16) – call then.

Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/zaplanuj_swoja_wizyte

Can I bring a guide dog?

Yes, assistance and guide dogs are welcome in the building and all rooms. There are no tactile maps, Braille room signs or audio navigation; the lifts have Braille and voice announcements.

Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/deklaracja_dostepnosci

Is there an induction loop?

According to the accessibility statement an induction loop is installed in the building, but it does not say in which hall or area. Please check with the box office (+48 91 431 07 20) whether it covers your seat.

Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/deklaracja_dostepnosci

Is there a dress code?

Wear whatever you like. Some come dressed up, others straight from work. Only the orchestra has a strict dress code.

Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/przewodnik_dla_nowych_sluchaczy

Is there an interval and how long is it?

Most symphony concerts have an interval, usually 20–30 minutes (mostly 30 minutes in 2026/2027); chamber and world music concerts usually have none. Duration and interval are shown for each event.

Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/przewodnik_dla_nowych_sluchaczy

When should I arrive?

At least 20 minutes before the concert. All public areas (cloakroom, cafés, galleries) open one hour before, the hall itself 30 minutes before.

Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/przewodnik_dla_nowych_sluchaczy
