Discover the Philharmonic

You do not need a concert ticket to come inside. Here is what you will find and why it is worth it.

1948

Orchestra

The Mieczysław Karłowicz Philharmonic Symphony Orchestra has been playing in Szczecin since 1948. 2026/2027 is its 79th artistic season.

On the programme: symphonic and chamber series, Monuments of the Symphony, FilHERmonia, jazz and world music, and in December the Karłowicz Fest devoted to its patron.

Symphonic concerts →

Building

Barozzi/Veiga · 2014

The white, ice-like form was designed by Barcelona studio Barozzi/Veiga, who won the 2007 competition against 44 practices from around the world. It stands on the site of the old Konzerthaus and opened with the 2014/2015 season.

In 2015 it received the European Union Prize for Contemporary Architecture – Mies van der Rohe Award.

Why come in

  • 01

    Acoustics

    The Symphony Hall’s geometry was developed with acoustician Dr Higini Arau; its parameters are compared to Vienna’s Musikverein.

  • 02

    Foyer and spiral stairs

    The tall white foyer with its spiral staircase is the most photographed interior in town.

  • 03

    Two galleries

    Level 4 Gallery and Art.Room. Free with a concert ticket.

  • 04

    Café

    Symfonia Cafe in the foyer is open on non-concert days too.

The building through the lens

Climb the foyer staircase: one photograph hangs on every turn. Halls, concerts and exhibitions, from the ground floor up to Galeria Poziom 4.
