      Quick answers

      Open only what you need.

      Info line · Tue–Thu 10:00–16:00+48 539 949 454
      How do I get there?

      Tram 1 stops right by the building (Filharmonia stop).

      Plac Rodła (about 5 min on foot) is served by trams 2, 3, 5, 10, 11, 12 and buses incl. 59, 68, 107, A, B, C.

      Live departures from both stops are shown below, straight from ZDiTM.

      Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/zaplanuj_swoja_wizyte

      Parking

      Underground car park with 154 spaces, entrance from ul. Małopolska 48. 2 accessible spaces on each level.

      Free for ticket holders. Opens one hour before the event.

      A lift takes you straight from the car park to the foyer.

      Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/zaplanuj_swoja_wizyte

      When should I arrive?

      At least 20 minutes before the concert. The building, cloakroom and cafés open an hour earlier; the hall opens 30 minutes before the start.

      Bells: one at 15 minutes, two at 10 minutes, three at 5 minutes before the start.

      Running late? Ushers will let you in at a suitable moment, sometimes only at the interval.

      Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/przewodnik_dla_nowych_sluchaczy

      Cloakroom

      In the foyer, straight ahead of the entrance.

      Open from one hour before the event until 30 minutes after it ends.

      Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/przewodnik_dla_nowych_sluchaczy

      Cafés

      Symfonia Cafe in the foyer: Tue–Fri 10:00–18:00, Sat–Sun 12:00–18:00. On concert days until the last guest.

      Concert café on the 3rd floor by the balcony entrance: from one hour before concerts with an interval until the end.

      No food or drinks in the concert halls.

      Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/zaplanuj_swoja_wizyte

      Accessibility

      Step-free main entrance; lifts to every floor and the car park (Braille and voice announcements).

      Wheelchair spaces in the front rows and side boxes of the Symphony Hall; ticket 10 zł, bookable online.

      Accessible toilets on the ground and 1st floor. Assistance and guide dogs welcome.

      Full accessibility information

      Source: filharmonia.szczecin.pl/deklaracja_dostepnosci

      With children

      Family concerts and the Young Music Lover Academy are for children 5+; RANIUTTO workshops welcome one-year-olds with a carer.

      For RANIUTTO you buy the child’s ticket online; the carer’s ticket is bought at the box office before the class.

      Leave the pram in the cloakroom; the lift serves every floor.
