Accessibility

What you can do comfortably here, what we do not have yet, and whom to contact. No vague promises.

Site settings

We remember them in this browser across all pages.

Theme
Text size

The same setting as in the “Accessibility tools” panel in the header. The site also works at 200% browser zoom.

  • Reduce motionTurns off transitions and movement.
  • Show the seagullThe seagull sits by the Assistant and offers a hint now and then. Turn it off if it distracts you.

What we have

Events with access features in the calendar

  • Step-free entrance

    The main entrance on ul. Małopolska is level with the pavement.

  • Lifts everywhere

    To every floor and the car park. Braille buttons and voice announcements.

  • Wheelchair spaces

    Symphony Hall: 4 spaces in the front rows and side boxes. Ticket 10 zł, bookable online together with a companion seat. Chamber Hall: book via the box office.

  • Accessible toilets

    On the ground floor and 1st floor.

  • Parking

    2 accessible spaces on each level, lift straight to the foyer.

  • Assistance dogs

    Assistance and guide dogs are welcome in the building and the halls.

  • Induction loop

    Installed in the building. The statement does not say which hall: ask the box office before buying.

  • Tours with PJM

    Selected Friday tours with a Polish Sign Language interpreter.

    Next tours with PJM: 6 November, 15:00 · 4 December, 15:00 · Sight-seeing the Philharmonic in Polish (with PJM interpretation)

  • Staff support

    Staff are trained. On request we provide an assistant during the concert: ask the box office.

  • Sensory information

    Every event lists loudness, lighting, duration and interval.

Reduced tickets: people with a disability certificate (children under 16 and severe disability: 10 zł; mild or moderate: reduced ticket). Companion: reduced ticket.

Seniors: daytime concerts, 60+ tickets, getting here step by step

What we do not have yet

We say it openly so nobody is surprised on arrival.

  • —tactile maps or Braille room signs
  • —an audio navigation system in the building
  • —contrast markings on the glass entrance doors and step edges
  • —audio description of concerts
  • —a permanent PJM interpreter (outside selected tours)
  • —captions on every video on the website
  • —fully accessible PDF programmes: so we also publish programmes as web pages
  • —an online seat plan of the Chamber Hall

Source: Philharmonic accessibility statement, last reviewed 18.03.2026
